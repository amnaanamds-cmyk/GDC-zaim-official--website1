Grading scheme

Regulations

Academic rules

Attendance requirement
A minimum of 75% attendance in each course is required to appear in the terminal examination. Attendance is recorded by the course teacher and is visible to students in the portal, where a warning is generated once attendance falls below the threshold.
Assessment weighting
Sessional work (assignments, quizzes and presentations) carries 25%, the mid-term examination 25% and the final examination 50% of the total marks for a course, unless the university notifies otherwise for a specific programme.
Semester promotion
A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.00 to be promoted. A student falling below this is placed on academic probation and is counselled by the departmental adviser.
Re-appearing in a course
A course in which an F grade is obtained must be repeated. The better grade is recorded, and the attempt history is retained in the academic record.
Result rectification
An application for rechecking may be submitted to the Controller of Examinations within 15 days of the result announcement, together with the prescribed fee.

Examination rules

  • The college identity card must be carried to every examination; candidates without it will not be admitted to the hall.
  • Roll number slips are issued only to students meeting the 75% attendance requirement and clear of outstanding dues.
  • Mobile phones, smart watches and programmable devices are not permitted in the examination hall.
  • Use of unfair means is dealt with under the university’s disciplinary regulations.

Check your result

Enter your registration number to view the semester result published by the examination office. Students can also see the full academic record, GPA trend and downloadable transcript in the student portal.

Format: year–GDCZ–department code–roll number

Results are published only after verification by the Controller of Examinations. For a correction, apply for rechecking within 15 days of notification.
