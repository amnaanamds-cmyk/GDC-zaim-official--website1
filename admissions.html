Admissions 2026

Admission to all programmes is strictly on merit, calculated from academic record according to the weighting notified by the Higher Education Department.

Current status — second merit list displayed

Candidates on the second merit list must deposit dues and complete enrolment at the Admission Office by 22 September 2026. Seats not confirmed by the deadline will be offered to candidates on the next list. Download the merit list (PDF).

How to apply

  1. Check eligibility. Confirm that you meet the minimum marks and subject requirements for the programme, listed in the table below.
  2. Obtain the prospectus and form. Download both from the Downloads section, or collect them from the Admission Office.
  3. Complete the application. Fill in the form, attach attested copies of all required documents and affix recent photographs.
  4. Submit before the deadline. Submit at the Admission Office, or through the online form below once the admissions portal is live.
  5. Track your application. Use the reference number issued on submission to check the status of your application at any stage.
  6. Confirm your seat. If selected, deposit the dues and complete enrolment within the notified period.

      Admission FAQs

      Can I apply for more than one programme?
      Yes. A single application form allows up to three programme preferences in order of priority. Merit is assessed separately for each programme, and a seat is offered against the highest preference for which you qualify.
      Is there an entry test?
      An entry test is conducted for BS Computer Science and BS Physics where applications exceed twice the sanctioned seats. The test date is notified on the notice board and by SMS to registered applicants.
      What if my name does not appear on the merit list?
      Subsequent merit lists are displayed as seats remain vacant after each enrolment deadline. Keep checking the notice board and this website until the admission process closes.
      Are fee concessions available?
      Yes. Need-based scholarships, Zakat assistance and merit concessions are available. See the scholarships page for the schemes currently open.
      Can I apply if my result is awaited?
      Applications are accepted on a provisional basis with a result-awaited certificate, but admission is confirmed only after the detailed marks certificate is submitted and verified.
