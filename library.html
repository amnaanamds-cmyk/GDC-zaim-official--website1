Borrowing rules

  • Three books at a time for 14 days, renewable once if not reserved.
  • Overdue fine of PKR 5 per day per book.
  • Reference books and periodicals are for reading-hall use only.
  • Library clearance is required before transcripts and migration certificates are issued.
