Scholarships & Financial Assistance

How to apply

  1. Download the application form for the relevant scheme from the Downloads section.
  2. Attach the guardian’s income certificate, the previous result card and a copy of the CNIC/B-Form.
  3. Submit the completed file to the Scholarship Cell before the notified deadline.
  4. Attend the verification interview if called by the scrutiny committee.
  5. Selected candidates are notified on the notice board and through the student portal.

Scholarship Cell: Administration Block, Room 12 · Mon – Thu, 09:00 – 13:00 · scholarships@gdczaim.edu.pk

Questions about scholarships

Can I hold more than one scholarship?
Federal and provincial schemes generally do not permit a student to hold two full scholarships at the same time. A college fee concession may be combined with a stipend-only award; the Scholarship Cell will advise on individual cases.
What income documentation is accepted?
A salary certificate for salaried guardians, or an income certificate issued by the relevant revenue official for self-employed and agricultural households. An affidavit alone is not sufficient.
When are scholarship funds disbursed?
External scheme funds are disbursed directly by the sponsoring body, usually within eight to twelve weeks of final approval. College concessions are applied to the fee challan for the relevant semester.
Does a scholarship continue automatically?
Continuation depends on maintaining the CGPA and attendance conditions of the scheme. Renewal is processed at the start of each academic year.
