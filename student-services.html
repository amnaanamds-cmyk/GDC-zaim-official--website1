Student Services

Request certificates and official documents, and track each request from submission to delivery.

Services offered

All requests are processed by the Registrar’s office. Turnaround times are counted from the date on which a complete request, with dues cleared, is received.

How a request is processed

Every request moves through five recorded stages. The student sees the current stage in the portal, and the office records who acted at each step.

    Before you apply: clear outstanding library books and fee dues. Requests with pending dues are held at the review stage until clearance is recorded.
