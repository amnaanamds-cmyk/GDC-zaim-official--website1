General enquiry

Use this form for admissions, academic or general questions. Enquiries are routed to the relevant office and answered during working hours.

Complaints & feedback

Complaints are routed to the responsible department and tracked through Submitted → Under Review → Resolved. You receive a reference number on submission, and anonymous feedback is accepted without contact details.

Anonymous complaints are investigated, but we cannot report the outcome back to you.
Getting here

Location & directions

The campus is on Main Campus Road, about two kilometres from the town bus stand. College buses serve four routes covering the town and adjoining union councils.

  • By college bus: four routes, departing between 07:00 and 07:30.
  • By public transport: local vans from the main bus stand stop at the college gate.
  • By car: visitor parking is available inside the main gate.
