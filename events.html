Participation

Registering for an event

Registration for most events is open to all enrolled students and is handled by the organising department or society. Where online registration is enabled, students register through the portal and receive a check-in code; participation certificates are issued after the event.

  • Team events require the department’s sports or society representative to submit the team list.
  • Attendance at seminars and workshops is recorded against the student’s co-curricular record.
  • Certificates are generated from the participant list and published in the student portal.
